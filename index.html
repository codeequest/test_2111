☕ Coffee Around the World

Same bean, very different traditions.

Italy

Espresso

Short and strong, usually drunk standing at the bar in a minute or two. Cappuccino is mostly a morning drink.

Turkey

Türk Kahvesi

Finely ground coffee simmered in a small pot called a cezve and served unfiltered, with the grounds settling at the bottom of the cup.

Vietnam

Cà Phê Sữa Đá

Dark-roast coffee dripped through a small metal phin filter over sweetened condensed milk, then poured over ice.

Ethiopia

Buna Ceremony

Beans are roasted, ground and brewed in a jebena clay pot in front of guests. It's a social ritual that can last hours.

Morocco

Spiced Coffee

Often brewed with spices such as cinnamon, black pepper, ginger or nutmeg for a warm, aromatic cup.

Sweden

Fika

Less a drink than a habit: a daily break for coffee and something sweet, often a cinnamon bun, shared with friends or coworkers.

Did you know? Legend says coffee was discovered in Ethiopia by a goat herder named Kaldi, who noticed his goats became energetic after eating red berries from a certain shrub.
